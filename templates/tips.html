Scam Safety Tips

Scammers use different tricks to steal money, passwords and personal information. Learn how to recognize common warning signs and protect yourself.

🔐

Never Share Your OTP

Never share your OTP, PIN, password or banking information with anyone. Banks and legitimate companies will never ask for your OTP over a call or message.

🔗

Check Suspicious Links

Before opening a link, check the website address carefully. Strange domains, unusual spellings and shortened links can be warning signs.

⚠️

Beware of Urgent Messages

Scammers often create panic by saying your account will be blocked or your offer will expire soon. Stop and verify before taking action.

💳

Verify Before Paying

Always verify the person, business or website before sending money. Do not make payments simply because someone is pressuring you.

🎁

Beware of Fake Prizes

If someone tells you that you have won a prize but asks you to pay a fee first, be careful. Unexpected prize messages are commonly used in scams.

📢

Report Scams

If you encounter a suspicious website, message or transaction, report it to the appropriate authorities and warn others.

🛡️ Remember: Stop. Check. Then Pay.

When something feels suspicious, don't rush. Take a moment to verify the information before clicking a link, sharing personal details or sending money.
