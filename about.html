About FraudX Scam Detector

🛡️ FraudX Scam Detector is a safety tool designed to help people identify possible scam and fraud warning signs before making a payment.

🔍 What does it do?

FraudX Scam Detector checks information such as suspicious messages, website links, transaction amounts and delivery details.

🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to help users think twice before sending money or sharing sensitive information with unknown people or websites.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Never share your OTP, password, PIN or other confidential information with someone you do not trust.
